The web was conceived as a federated network of independent nodes. Over the past fifteen years, an immense amount of creative and intellectual output has been funneled into walled gardens.

## The Cost of Rented Real Estate

When you publish exclusively on third-party platforms, you do not own your audience, your styling, or your data pipeline. Platform rules, monetization models, and algorithmic filters can alter overnight without recourse.

> "A sovereign publisher controls their storage, their domain, and their presentation layer."

## Static Publishing as Digital Sovereignty

By adopting static generation engines like Notesby deployed to your own infrastructure:

- You retain 100% ownership of your Markdown source files.
- Your publication can be deployed to any cloud provider or server in seconds.
- Zero vendor lock-in safeguards your intellectual archive indefinitely.
